3NestWorth mobile — every screen at 390pt, five bottom tabs

The model. Five tabs — Overview, Trends, Update (centre, the one thing you'll do standing at a bank app), Plan, More. Manage screens (Accounts, Income, Transactions, Settings, Import) live under More. Wide tables become stacked cards; comparison tables keep their label column pinned and scroll horizontally. Every tap target is ≥44px.
